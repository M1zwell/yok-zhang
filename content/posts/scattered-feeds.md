dseek is a Data Intelligence Platform. The point is not another dashboard. The world already publishes itself — scattered feeds, APIs, datasets, maps — and almost none of it is seekable in one place.

Scattered is the default. A terminal. A Hong Kong map. A research tab. Held together. Made seekable.

That is the work: not to invent a new pile of data, but to make the existing pile answer when you ask.

Open the [dseek terminal](https://dseek.ai/terminal). Ask it something that used to take five tabs.

The public surface is [dseek.ai](https://dseek.ai). Signup lives at [dseek.ai/signup](https://dseek.ai/signup). The map of the city is a sibling, not a plugin: [Yok-Iso HK](https://dseek.ai/hk).
