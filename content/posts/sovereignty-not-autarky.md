AI主权超周期. The sovereign AI supercycle.

Sovereignty is not autarky. It is not building the whole stack from sand to software. Sovereignty is control of key dependencies so the system still runs, audits, and migrates when outside relations worsen.

The dependencies: compute, data, energy, models, talent. Must control: location, access, exit path. Can cooperate: apps, tools, the global ecosystem.

Fragmentation did not kill the AI wave. It turned one global buildout into many regional rebuilds. Regional copies times energy constraint times trusted localisation. The buildout multiplied.

Value moves up the stack toward the control plane. Sovereign cloud, security, scheduling, compliance. Enterprise last mile: compute is "can it run"; data and governance is "can it scale."

Invest for sovereign premium. Order visibility times pricing power times sovereignty correlation.
