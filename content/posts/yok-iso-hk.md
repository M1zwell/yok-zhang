Yok-Iso HK is an isometric, pixel Hong Kong. You can walk the harbour in a browser tab.

The buildings are not invented. Building data © The Government of the Hong Kong SAR (Lands Department), via DATA.GOV.HK. Rendered and stylised by Jubit. Derived work; not for resale as a standalone dataset.

Central. Tsim Sha Tsui. Mong Kok. Sha Tin. Tsuen Wan. Pick a place — the city is already there.

Hong Kong is not a backdrop. It is the map.

Open [dseek.ai/hk](https://dseek.ai/hk). From this garden, the Tools page jumps districts without leaving the thought.
