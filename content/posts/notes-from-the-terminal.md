Writing lives in the [dseek terminal, research tab](https://dseek.ai/terminal?tab=research). That is the live surface — not a CMS, not a draft folder that never ships.

This garden keeps a local copy of what is worth pinning: AI coding, maps, the products that are actually up.

AI coding, here, means the thing compiles and the thing is used. Agents, terminals, maps of Hong Kong, tiny planets of other cities. The research tab is the desk. These pages are the ones that left the desk.

If you want the live writing, open the frame below — or skip the frame and [open the research tab](https://dseek.ai/terminal?tab=research) in its own window. A symbol, if you want one: [00700](https://dseek.ai/terminal?tab=research&symbol=00700).
