Some of it writes down. Most of it does not.

We know more than we can tell. A garden can hold the sentences that survived the desk. The rest is tacit: you walk a city on a tiny planet, you jump a district in Yok-Iso HK, you open the terminal and ask. No paragraph substitutes for that.

If a tool is live, use it. Reading is the remainder that fit on a page.
