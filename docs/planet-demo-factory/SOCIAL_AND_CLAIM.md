### What LIVE must mean on-screen (we have the clip)

Use `06-highlights/03_live_data_pin_weather_parking.mp4` plus the pin still.

**Can say live / near-live:** OSM footprints on the planet; weather chips (Day/Night/Rain/Typhoon/Undersea); pin card with coords, **27°C / AQI / UV**, car parks, nearby POIs, “live data: HK gov open data”.

**Cannot say live:** landmarks still in PR, SSO, pay-to-win, GPS “Ask to be located” as live location.

**Never:** fake the locate confirm. We skipped it.

So: **yes** to “playable city planet + live data”; **no** to unbacked “world’s first” unless you later scope it tightly (still soft).
