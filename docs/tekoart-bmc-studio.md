## Longer about
I build a small family of tools and worlds from Mong Kok:

- **tekoart.com** — A case you dress. A card you change. Original Juju / Pekomon window cases (63×88), made to order in Hong Kong.
- **ichina.co** — The garden: writing, tools, and free walks through what we make.
- **jubuddy.com/planet** — Walk a real city as a tiny planet.
- **dseek.ai** — Research terminal, football academy reminders, morning brief.
- **gozayden.com** — Academy venues and clips for the next generation.
- **gghere.com** — Worlds you can step into.
- **jubit.ai** — One sign-in for the whole family.

Support here funds hosting, prints, and the next sketch. Thank you.
